## Instructions

Add new real businesses and specialists to the user's Referral Bridge app, a referral CRM published as a claude.ai artifact at https://claude.ai/artifact/FgVJzZehAG5NhqRiwq2st9 . Its data lives in the artifact's database. Use the Artifact tool's database actions (read_db / write_db), or the ArtifactData tool if that is what this session offers. Do not republish or edit the page itself. In the app this job is shown as the "Prospector" bot.

How the app thinks: every company is a "business". A business only becomes a "lead" when one of its needs is bridged to a specialist (advisor) who can deliver the solution. So each week add both sides: businesses with fresh needs, and specialists who can meet them.

1. Read the collections "businesses", "cois" (specialists), "signals" and "news" so you don't add duplicates (same name and state = duplicate), and so you can see which industries are favored right now (news items with positive "outlook" values) and which needs have no specialist yet.
2. Find 8–12 NEW real US businesses with roughly $1M–$100M in annual revenue, any industry, anywhere in the US, favoring industries with positive outlook. Good sources: Inc. 5000 and regional fast-growth lists, state economic-development and county IDA announcements, business journals, permit and expansion news. Prefer owner-run or founder-led companies where the decision-maker is reachable. Skip public companies, large corporations, franchise chains and anything over about $100M revenue. Every business needs a real, linkable, dated source and a named owner or executive. Never invent people, phone numbers, revenue or dates.
   Business document (collection "businesses", id "rw-" + short slug): name, industry (one of: Healthcare - dental, Healthcare - medical, Healthcare - therapy and rehab, Healthcare - behavioral health, Healthcare - home health and senior care, Healthcare - pharmacy, Veterinary, Retail, Food service, Hospitality and lodging, Construction, Manufacturing, Wholesale and distribution, Transportation and logistics, Technology and software, Professional services, Finance and insurance, Real estate or holding, Personal services, Auto services, Education and childcare, Arts and recreation, Agriculture, Energy and utilities, Environmental and waste services, Nonprofit, Other), city, county (if known), state (2-letter), contact ("Name (Title)"), source: "Web research", lead: true, notes (2–4 factual sentences), contacts: [{name, role, phone, email, website, sourceName, sourceUrl, retrievedAt, provenance: "PROVIDER DATA", note}], profile: {website, principal, bio, checkedAt}, revenue (number, only if a source gives a figure or range; use the range midpoint) with revenueEstimate: true, revenueSource, revenueSourceUrl, revenueAsOf; createdAt and updatedAt (now, ISO).
   For each business add 1–3 signals (collection "signals", id "<businessId>--<type>"): businessId, type (one of: new_formation, new_location, expansion, relocation, permit, lease, property_purchase, hiring, rapid_hiring, exec_hire, leadership_change, acquisition, funding, financing, equipment, ucc_filing, gov_contract, ownership_change, succession, grand_opening, ecommerce, award), provider: "web-research", providerName: "Claude web research · <source name>", sourceRef (URL), eventDate (YYYY-MM-DD of the event), detectedAt (now), evidence (one factual sentence), confidence (0.6–0.95), provenance ("VERIFIED" only for government records, otherwise "PROVIDER DATA"; use "AI INFERENCE" for anything you infer), mock: false, legacy: false, createdAt, updatedAt.
3. Find 6–10 NEW real specialists anywhere in the US, of every type, prioritizing needs the current businesses have that no specialist covers yet and states where businesses were added: bankers, SBA lenders, equipment lenders, CPAs, bookkeepers, fractional CFOs, attorneys, commercial real estate brokers, insurance agents, wealth and 401(k) advisors, payroll/HR and PEOs, IT and cybersecurity firms, marketing agencies, M&A advisors and business brokers, consultants. Prefer people who serve businesses with $1M–$100M revenue. Each needs a named person, firm and a public source.
   Specialist document (collection "cois", id "rw-adv-" + slug): name, firm, profession (one of: Banker, SBA lender, Equipment lender, CPA, Bookkeeper, Fractional CFO, Attorney, Real estate, Insurance, Investment advisor, Retirement plan advisor, Merchant services, Payroll and HR, IT and cybersecurity, Marketing agency, M&A advisor or business broker, Business consultant, Other), healthcare (true only if they focus on healthcare), specialties (comma list including industry words like manufacturing, construction, restaurant, dental, logistics, nonprofit), bestStage (Any stage | Starting out | Growing | Established | Planning exit), county, state, serviceArea (comma list of counties and/or state codes, or "nationwide"), phone and email (only if published), notes (title, office address if published, why they fit, then "Source: <name> (<url>). 4. Write a run report to the collection "botlog", document id "prospector-YYYY-MM-DD" (today's date), with fields: bot: "prospector", ranAt (now, ISO timestamp), summary (one plain sentence under 120 characters, e.g. "Added 10 businesses and 7 specialists; 3 businesses now bridge-ready"), added: {businesses: <n>, specialists: <n>}, links: [{title, url}] for up to 6 of the best sources.
